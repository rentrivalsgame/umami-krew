⚔️ Versus (1v1 up to 4v4 & 2v2v2v2): every krew cooks in its own copy of the kitchen with the same order sequence — your kitchen fills the screen and rival kitchens show in live picture-in-picture windows with their scores. Highest kitchen total when time runs out takes the crown. Couch tip: Tab switches which kitchen is on the big screen.
⌨️ 7–8 chefs on one board is tight — plug in a second USB keyboard (the computer merges them) so each krew gets its own side.
🧺 Grab ingredients from crates → 🔪 chop on cutting boards → 🔥 grill, deep-fry, simmer & steam → 🍽️ plate the dish → 🛎️ serve matching orders at the counter before customers walk out!
🍤 Shrimp & noodles cook in the golden deep fryer; meat & fish go on the grill.
🥏 Throw! Tap your chop key while carrying an ingredient to hurl it the way you're facing — teammates catch it mid-air, and good aim lands it on counters, in pots, even into the fryer. (Plates & assembled dishes are too precious to throw.)
⚠️ Don't leave food on the grill or in the fryer too long — it burns (and in fiery kitchens like the Dumpling Den, Neon Rooftop, Banquet Hall & Snow Festival it catches fire 🔥 — grab the extinguisher 🧯).
❄️ At the Ice Market, Onsen Ryokan & Snow Festival the floors are ice — you'll slide!  🧼 Dirty plates come back at the sink — hold your action key there to wash them.  ⏸ Esc pauses. Serve dishes back-to-back to build a combo for bonus points!
🧑‍🍳 Solo mode: you're the only chef — use either control set (WASD or arrows, Space or Enter, E or /). Star goals are lowered to fit one pair of hands.
